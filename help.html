Frequently Asked Questions

How do I withdraw my Referral Wallet balance?

Your Referral Wallet unlocks once its balance reaches ₦1,000, built up from referral commissions you've earned. Once unlocked, tap Withdraw on the Referral Wallet card.

How do referral commissions work?

Share your referral link from "My Referrals." Once the person you referred buys their first ticket (min ₦100), you start earning 10% commission for the next 90 days on tickets they buy using their Main Wallet — up to ₦1,000 total per referral. Tickets they pay for using their own Referral Wallet balance don't earn you commission. Commission is credited straight to your Referral Wallet and can be used to buy tickets anytime, even before it's unlocked for withdrawal.

When does the daily draw happen?

Every day at 8:00PM. The number of winners depends on how many entries were active that day (roughly 1 winner per 10 entries). Every ₦100 you spend is a separate entry with the same fixed prize — buying a bigger tier just means more entries, so more chances to win, not a bigger single prize. If you bought several entries, more than one could win.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed by our team. You'll see the status update in your Transactions history.

Is my money safe?

All balance changes happen through secured, server-side checks — no client can alter a balance directly. If anything looks wrong, contact support above.

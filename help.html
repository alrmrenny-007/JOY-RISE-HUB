Frequently Asked Questions

How do I withdraw my Referral Wallet balance?

Your Referral Wallet unlocks once its balance reaches ₦3,000 — this includes your ₦1,000 signup credit plus any referral commissions. Once unlocked, tap Withdraw on the Referral Wallet card.

How do referral commissions work?

Share your referral link from "My Referrals." When someone signs up with it and buys enough tickets to cross the activation threshold, you earn a commission credited straight to your Referral Wallet.

When does the daily draw happen?

Every day at 8:00PM. One winning ticket is picked from everyone who bought a ticket that day, and the prize depends on which tier their ticket was.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal requests are reviewed and processed by our team. You'll see the status update in your Transactions history.

Is my money safe?

All balance changes happen through secured, server-side checks — no client can alter a balance directly. If anything looks wrong, contact support above.
